The Problem

Fine-tuning is still
unreasonably hard.

  • Building training datasets from your own documents requires custom pipelines and manual labor. Teams spend weeks on data preparation alone.
  • Setting up GPU infrastructure, configuring training runs, and managing hyperparameters demands deep ML expertise. Most teams don't have it.
  • Evaluating whether your fine-tuned model actually outperforms the base model is ad-hoc and inconsistent. No standardized benchmarking.
  • Deploying and distributing models across your organization involves yet another set of tools. Fragmented workflows slow everything down.
02
The Solution

One platform.
Upload to deploy.

1 unified platform replaces a fragmented stack of tools, scripts, and notebooks
0 ML expertise required — upload your documents and ecole handles the rest
∞ models you can train, evaluate, and push to HuggingFace Hub from one place

ecole automates dataset generation, GPU training, benchmarking, and model distribution — so your team can focus on the problem, not the plumbing.
